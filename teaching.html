Teaching & Mentoring

Geography through evidence and application.

I teach students to connect conceptual frameworks, environmental processes, spatial evidence, and decision-making in applied geographic problems.

Approach

From methods to geographic reasoning.

Technical fluency matters most when students understand why a method is appropriate, what assumptions it carries, and how spatial evidence connects to a larger geographic question.

Across introductory environmental courses, GIS instruction, and advanced seminars, I emphasize reproducible analysis, interpretation, clear communication, and the integration of physical and social dimensions of environmental problems.

Mentoring

Applied, transparent, collaborative.

I approach mentoring as a progression from question formation to reproducible analysis and confident interpretation.

Students are encouraged to understand the provenance and limitations of data, document workflows clearly, test assumptions, and communicate results to audiences beyond the classroom.
