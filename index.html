About

Environmental process, spatial evidence, and consequential landscapes.

Sam Chambers is an environmental geographer and biometeorologist whose research examines how environmental conditions, infrastructure, and governance shape human and ecological exposure, mobility, and survival.

His work combines spatial analysis, environmental modeling, remote sensing, and process-based approaches across climate and health, border studies, and landscape ecology.
